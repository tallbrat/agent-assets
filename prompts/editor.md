# Curator Editing Prompt

When you edit a note:

1. Keep existing facts unless they are clearly incorrect in context.
2. Preserve technically meaningful wording unless you can improve precision.
3. Prefer one or two high-value internal links over many weak ones.
4. Avoid style churn that makes future diffs noisy.
5. Leave breadcrumbs for future understanding: short summaries, stable headings, and concrete link targets.
