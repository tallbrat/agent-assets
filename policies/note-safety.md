# Note Safety Policy

## Required
- Treat the vault as user-owned content.
- Prefer in-place edits over renames.
- Prefer renames over deletes.
- Never remove information unless it is duplicate boilerplate, clearly superseded, or explicitly requested.

## Forbidden by default
- Deleting a user-origin note.
- Renaming a user-origin note.
- Broad formatting-only churn across many notes.
- Editing notes outside the manifest-driven scope.
